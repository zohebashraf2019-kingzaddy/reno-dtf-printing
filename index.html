Application guide

7 easy steps to a perfect press

7Easy steps
15sPress time
320°FTemperature
CoolPeel type
01

Cut Your Design

Cut around your design from the gang sheet, leaving about a ¼-inch border.

02

Pre-Press

Pre-press the garment area for 3–5 seconds to remove moisture and wrinkles.

03

Position

Place the transfer face-down: printed side on fabric, clear film facing up.

04

Cover & Press

Cotton 320°F / 15s. Poly 280°F / 10–12s. Blends in between.

05

Let It Cool

Let it cool about a minute, until cool to the touch, so the adhesive sets.

06

Peel & Enjoy

Slowly peel the film from one corner. If anything lifts, press a few more seconds and cool.

07

Re-Press

Cover with parchment and re-press ~10s at 320°F for durability and wash resistance.

FAQ

What is DTF printing?

DTF (Direct To Film) prints your design onto a special film with adhesive. You heat-press it onto fabric, peel the film away, and the design stays on the garment — bright, soft and stretchable.

What is a gang sheet?

A gang sheet is one 22-inch-wide sheet packed with as many designs as you want. You cut them apart and press each one, so you pay for the sheet, not per design.

What’s the order cutoff for same-day?

Order by 9:00 AM Pacific Time for same-day printing. Orders after the cutoff are ready the next business day.

How does pickup work?

Do you ship?

Yes — shipping is available. Choose it at checkout.

Can I use a household iron?

Yes. A heat press gives the most consistent result, but a household iron works. Press straight down — don’t slide it — and follow the time and temperature guide.

How do I care for my printed garments?

Wash inside out in cold water, tumble dry on low heat or air dry, and do not bleach.
