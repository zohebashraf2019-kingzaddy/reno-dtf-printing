How to press your DTF transfers

Everything you need for a clean, long-lasting press — in 7 easy steps.

7Easy steps
15sPress time

What you’ll need

Heat press or iron

A heat press gives the most even results; a household iron works too.

Scissors

Sharp scissors for clean edges around each design.

Your fabric

T-shirt, hoodie, tote bag — cotton, poly or blends.

Parchment paper

To cover the transfer and protect it during pressing.

Apply your transfer

Step by step

01

Cut Your Design

Cut around your design from the gang sheet, leaving about a ¼-inch border.

Pro tip: Use sharp scissors for clean edges.

02

Pre-Press

Pre-press the garment area for 3–5 seconds to remove moisture and wrinkles.

Pro tip: Helps the transfer adhere better and last longer.

03

Position

Place the transfer face-down: printed side on fabric, clear film facing up.

Pro tip: Measure twice, press once.

04

Cover & Press

Cotton 320°F / 15s. Poly 280°F / 10–12s. Blends in between.

Pro tip: Don’t slide the iron — press straight down.

05

Let It Cool

Let it cool about a minute, until cool to the touch, so the adhesive sets.

Pro tip: Don’t rush the cooling step.

06

Peel & Enjoy

Slowly peel the film from one corner. If anything lifts, press a few more seconds and cool.

Pro tip: Peel smooth and steady.

07

Re-Press

Cover with parchment and re-press ~10s at 320°F for durability and wash resistance.

Pro tip: This final press locks it all in.
